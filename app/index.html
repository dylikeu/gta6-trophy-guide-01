      Why almost nothing can be missed

      • All 12 story trophies unlock automatically and cannot be missed.
      • Every trophy-related quest giver is still available after the story in free-roam.
      • Optional missions that expire (black icons with pink letters) do not matter for trophies — there are 200–300 of them and the 100% checklist needs any 100.
      • Nothing on the 100% completion checklist is missable; everything is doable after the story.
      • Cheats disable trophies and saving — keep them off. There is no mission replay, so keep manual saves at the start and end of each chapter (15 slots).
